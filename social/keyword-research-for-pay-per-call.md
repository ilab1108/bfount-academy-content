# LinkedIn post

In pay per call, a keyword that gets lots of clicks can still lose you money.

You're paid for billable calls, not traffic.

Score every keyword before you spend:
Intent: do they want to act?
Fit: do they match the buyer's rules?
Urgency: do they need help today?
Risk: could they think you're the government or a brand?

Start on exact and phrase match. Add negatives on day one. Judge keywords by billable calls.
