"Exact match" in Google Ads hasn't meant exact for years.

[emergency plumber] can match "emergency plumbers", "emergancy plumber" and even "urgent plumber".
Broad match modifier (+keyword) was retired in 2021. Old entries now run as broad.
When several of your keywords match one search, an identical exact keyword gets first claim.
So if you know your best converting searches, add them as exact keywords.
