A low Quality Score is a tax on every click.

Google rates each keyword 1–10 on three things:
Expected CTR. Ad relevance. Landing page experience.

Each one is marked Above average, Average or Below average. Fix the one that says "Below average" first.

Tight ad groups fix relevance. Pages that keep the ad's promise fix landing page experience.

And don't chase 10/10. Fix the keywords that spend the most money first.

Our new guide includes a triage table: match your three ratings to the first fix.
