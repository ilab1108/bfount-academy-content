Your Google Ads bid strategy only optimizes for the goal you give it.

Tell it to chase clicks and it buys cheap clicks.
Count page views as conversions and it buys page views.

The order that works for most accounts:
Manual CPC or Maximize clicks → Maximize conversions → add a target CPA.

Start your target near the CPA you get today, then tighten it step by step.

And note: Enhanced CPC is gone from Search and Display since March 2025.
