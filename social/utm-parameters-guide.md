Your paid Instagram ads might be hiding inside "Organic Social."

If the link says utm_medium=social, GA4 counts the click as organic — even though you paid for it.

Three tags fix most campaign tracking:
utm_source = where the click came from
utm_medium = what kind of traffic it is
utm_campaign = why you sent it

Lowercase everything. Never tag your own internal links. Keep one shared sheet.
