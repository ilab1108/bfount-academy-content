"What's a good cost per lead?" is the wrong first question.

Start here instead:
CAC = acquisition cost ÷ new customers
LTV = monthly revenue × gross margin × lifetime
Payback = CAC ÷ monthly gross profit

Then work backwards: max CPL = the CAC you'll accept × close rate.
$600 × 20% close rate = you can pay up to $120 per lead.
