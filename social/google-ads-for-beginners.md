Most first Google Ads campaigns fail before they launch.

Not because of the bid. Because of the setup.

• No conversion tracking — so nothing to learn from
• Broad match on a brand-new account
• No negative keywords ("jobs", "free", "DIY" eat the budget)
• Every click sent to the homepage
• A budget picked from thin air instead of your max cost per lead

Work backwards: profit per customer × close rate = the most you can pay for a lead.
